# THE HOLLOW MOON — story bible (draft 1)

*Working story for Medieval Realm. Written around your 8 heroes. I invented the personalities and history from their names, titles and portraits, so change anything that doesn't match who they are to you. Everything below is already wired into the game unless marked (later).*

---

## 1. The one-paragraph version

Seven years ago, on the **Night of the Hollow Moon**, the Silver Moon Circle tried to seal something ancient called **the Hush** beneath the ruins above the village of **Lanternfall**. The seal broke, the moon went dark for a night, the Frost swallowed the high country, and the Circle's leader, **Archmage Ysolde Starweave**, vanished. Everyone in the village agrees it was "a terrible natural winter." Everyone is lying, or only half-remembering. The six people Nina can befriend each carry one piece of the truth, plus a guilt that stops them from sharing it. Nina, Ysolde's apprentice, survived the Night with her memory torn out and **a shard of the Hush sleeping inside her** — that's where her frost magic really comes from. The deeper she gets into people's trust, the more of the Night she gets back, and the more she has to decide who to forgive, who to protect, and who to tell.

**Tone:** cozy on the surface (lamps, animals, fishing, rain) with a grown-up, aching story underneath. Nobody is a villain; everybody made a reasonable choice that went terribly wrong.

## 2. The world

| Thing | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Lanternfall** | The village (Meadow Village on the map). Its lamp posts are not decoration — each holds a drop of moonlight and keeps the Hush quiet at night. They are slowly dimming. |
| **The Hush** | A cold, silent presence under the **Old Ruins / Ancient Arch**. It eats warmth and sound. It isn't evil; it is hungry and very, very old. Older than the elves who first sealed it. |
| **The Silver Moon Circle** | A small order of scholar-mages who studied the seal. Nina was the youngest (16). After the Night the Circle disbanded; most are dead or gone. |
| **The Order of the Blue Lion** | The Crown's holy knights. The **Lion's Watch** guarded the ritual. Their High Marshal, **Aldous Varrick**, secretly wanted to drain the Hush's power as a weapon. |
| **The Wolfclan** | Frostpeak herders and warriors. The Frost took their high pastures; they now live in a camp on the edge of the snow. |
| **The Moonveil elves** | The seal's original wardens. Most left the Realm. Elyra stayed. |
| **The Moonstone** | The seal's keystone, kept in the Circle's tower. Stolen two weeks before the Night. |
| **The Resonator** | A clockwork device built to "amplify the seal". It was really a siphon to pull the Hush's power into the Order's hands. |

## 3. What really happened (the truth timeline)

1. **Three weeks before:** Varrick tells Thorin the Circle needs a **Resonator** to strengthen the seal. Thorin builds it, taking the Moonstone's replacement housing as the core.
2. **Two weeks before:** Someone must supply the *real* Moonstone as the Resonator's heart. **Finn**, who brokers favours for coin, hires **Kai** to lift it from the Circle's tower. Kai thinks it's a rich collector's job. Finn thinks the same. Neither knows who the real client is.
3. **The Night:** The Circle begins the Moonward ritual at the Arch. The Lion's Watch (Capt. **Brennan**) ring the ruins. Varrick orders the Resonator switched on **early**. It doesn't strengthen the seal — it **pulls**. The Arch cracks. The Hush starts to wake.
4. **Brennan** sees the machine for what it is, tries to stop it, and is ordered to withdraw the Watch. He obeys (the one decision he cannot forgive himself for), then turns back alone and carries a half-frozen **Nina** out of the Arch. He never found Ysolde.
5. **Ysolde** did the only thing left: she **sealed the Hush into herself and into Nina**, splitting it so neither would break. Ysolde now sleeps *inside* the seal. Nina carries a shard, and lost the memory of that night in the binding.
6. **Elyra** was supposed to be at the Arch. She was late — Kai had asked her to meet him that night to confess something. She arrived to a collapsing ring of ice.
7. **Seraphina** treated the survivors at the chapel. A dying Lion soldier confessed that **the commander gave the order**. She wrote it down and has kept it sealed ever since.
8. **Raika's** younger brother **Hakon** guided the Circle up the ridge that night. He was taken by the Frost. Something that has his face has been sitting in an ice cave at the Wolfclan camp ever since — silent, not eating, not aging.
9. **Finn**, afterward, was paid by the Order to write **The Song of the Long Frost** — a ballad about a natural calamity. It's the official story. He has hated every performance since.
10. Varrick was recalled to the capital. He has never stopped wanting the Hush. **His herald arrives at first frost** (Act 3).

## 4. Nina

*Public:* the quiet goth mage who lives in the farmhouse by the road. Locals know she's "Ysolde's girl". They are a little afraid of her frost.

*Truth:* She remembers nothing of the Night. The cold in her is **the Hush** — it dreams, and it likes the animals, the lamps and the people she's begun to love. As the lamps weaken, it stirs.

*What the player does:* befriend people, collect **Memory Fragments** (each person's view of the Night), find hidden places, gather the **Seven Pieces** (the six villagers' secrets + Nina's own), and decide what to do at the Hollow Moon.

## 5. The cast (what each of them hides)

Each person has **Trust 0–5**. Talk daily, give gifts they like, and do their small requests to raise it. Each level unlocks a new conversation. Level 3 opens a **hidden place** and a **memory of the Night**; level 5 gives their **Piece**.

### 🛡 Sir Brennan Stoneward — Holy Knight of the Blue Lion (Watch Captain)
- **Public:** stiff, polite, runs the Lion's Watch post. Walks the village at dusk, checking the lamps.
- **Hidden:** he **obeyed the order to withdraw**, then went back for Nina. He keeps **Ysolde's circlet**, which he found in the snow. He still gets letters from Varrick.
- **Wound:** he thinks he killed Ysolde and abandoned her. Secretly he is still in love with Seraphina and writes her letters he never sends.
- **Trust unlocks:** the official version → a duty he can't talk about → the Night (he saved you) → the circlet and Varrick's letters → the confession about the order.
- **Place:** *The Watch Cellar* (Nina's first night is here — a rotating memory).
- **Piece:** Ysolde's Circlet.

### 🌅 Seraphina Lightbrook — Paladin of Dawn's Mercy
- **Public:** warm, tireless, runs the chapel infirmary. Everyone's favourite.
- **Hidden:** she holds the **sealed confession** naming Varrick. She also hides that her **healing light is failing**: the cold in her chest is real and getting worse. She doesn't tell anyone.
- **Wound:** she broke her engagement to **Brennan** because he lied about the Night. She doesn't know *why* he lied. She is protecting him (by staying silent) and punishing him (by leaving) at once.
- **Trust unlocks:** what the chapel does → her tiredness → the Night at the infirmary → the engagement story → the confession.
- **Place:** *The Quiet Room* (the chapel cellar where the survivors were treated).
- **Piece:** The Sealed Confession.

### 🌿 Elyra Moonveil — Ranger of the Whispering Woods
- **Public:** aloof elf, guardian of the forest, talks to animals more than people.
- **Hidden:** she is **over a hundred years old**. Her family were the seal's original wardens — the **Moonveil Seal** is her bloodline's job, and she walked away from it. Ysolde was her best friend (maybe more).
- **Wound:** she was **late** to the Arch because Kai asked her to meet him. She has never told Kai that's why she hates him. She doesn't trust magic users and she is slowly, grudgingly warming up to Nina.
- **Trust unlocks:** the forest's rules → she knew Ysolde → the old seal and what "Moonveil" means → her lateness → the second key.
- **Place:** *Moonveil Glade* (a treehouse near the Moon Well).
- **Piece:** The Moonveil Key.

### 🗡 Kai Nightshade — Shadow Rogue of Raven Cove
- **Public:** charming thief, shows up near lamp light at dusk, steals your apple, gives it back with interest.
- **Hidden:** he stole the **Moonstone** for a go-between. He didn't know. He's been sneaking into the Ruins at night ever since, trying to "undo it" and failing.
- **Wound:** the day after the Night he learned what he'd done. He'd asked **Elyra** to meet him to confess — then she hated him and he didn't know why. (She hates him for the right reason, wrong timing.)
- **Trust unlocks:** street-level charm → his cove → the theft → Elyra and the Night → who the go-between was.
- **Place:** *Raven Cove* (a cave on the Sunset Shore).
- **Piece:** The Moonstone Chip.

### 🎵 Finn Melodric — Wandering Bard of the Golden Tavern
- **Public:** the life of the Golden Tavern, loves a story, tells different versions of everything.
- **Hidden:** he was the **go-between** for the Moonstone job and wrote the official ballad **for money**. Since then, he's been secretly collecting the **real** stories in a hidden book, **The Honest Book**.
- **Wound:** he lost his friendship with **Thorin** when Thorin learned about the ballad. He flirts with everyone to avoid being known.
- **Trust unlocks:** a song for every mood → the ballad's real author → the job → his Honest Book → who paid him.
- **Place:** *The Tavern Back Room*.
- **Piece:** The Honest Book.

### ⚙ Thorin Grimbelt — Master Tinker and Forgekeeper
- **Public:** gruff dwarf, forge by day, tinkering by night, makes the **lamps** you walk by.
- **Hidden:** he built the **Resonator**, and later realised what it was. The **lamps** are his penance. Each one carries a drop of the moonlight he salvaged. They're weakening and he can't remake them without the Moonstone.
- **Wound:** estranged from **Finn** (ballad), distrusted by **Raika** (iron he sold the Order was used against her clan years ago), and he can't sleep.
- **Trust unlocks:** the lamp craft → what lamps really do → the Resonator → his cellar → the lamp's schematic.
- **Place:** *The Forge Cellar* (where the Resonator's remains are).
- **Piece:** The Resonator Schematic.

### 🐺 Raika Frostbane — Wolfclan Chieftain
- **Public:** fierce, suspicious of mages and the Lion Order, deeply loyal to her clan. Respects strength, loves a good fish.
- **Hidden:** the silent being in the ice cave is **Hakon-who-is-not-Hakon**. It doesn't hurt anyone. It seems to listen when someone sings. It's the only creature that can tell where the seal's heart lies.
- **Wound:** the Frost took her brother. She blames the Circle *and* Thorin and the Order. She's afraid of Nina's frost because it's the same cold.
- **Trust unlocks:** clan rules → what the Frost took → the Night's cost to the clan → the Ice Cave → Hakon.
- **Place:** *The Wolfclan Ice Cave*.
- **Piece:** Hakon's Echo.

## 6. The relationship web (the messy part)

| Pair | The knot |
|---|---|
| **Brennan ↔ Seraphina** | Former fiancés. He lied, she left. She still carries the proof he's innocent; he still carries the guilt he isn't. Each thinks the other is the one who should speak first. |
| **Kai ↔ Elyra** | Former partners and lovers. She hates him for the theft; he doesn't know she doesn't know the whole story either. |
| **Finn ↔ Thorin** | Old drinking partners. Finn wrote the false ballad; Thorin can't forgive being used. They still keep each other's cups. |
| **Raika ↔ Thorin** | Thorin's iron armed the Order against her clan years ago. She will not drink in the tavern when he's there. |
| **Raika ↔ Seraphina** | Seraphina healed Wolfclan children for free. Raika owes her more than she'll say. |
| **Brennan ↔ Raika** | The Lion Order burned a Wolfclan outpost when Brennan was a recruit. He's never apologised. |
| **Finn ↔ Kai** | Finn hired him for the job. Both blame themselves, neither says so. |
| **Elyra ↔ Ysolde** | Best friends, maybe more; unspoken for sixty years. |
| **Thorin ↔ Kai** | Kai once stole a wrench from the forge. Thorin has been trying to catch him for years. It's a joke. It isn't. |

**Telling secrets matters.** At several points the player learns something about one person from another. You can keep it, tell the person it concerns, or tell someone else. Each choice shifts trust and unlocks (or locks) later conversations.

## 7. The Seven Pieces and the story shape

| Act | Story clock | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Act 1 — Lanternfall** (now) | Day 1 onward | Meet everyone, earn trust 0–3, find the hidden places, collect Memory Fragments. |
| **Act 2 — The Honest Book** (now) | Trust 4–5 | The secrets between them come out; you decide what to share. Each person gives you their **Piece**. |
| **Act 3 — First Frost** (*later*) | Day 30 | Varrick's herald arrives with a Lion company. Everyone has to choose a side. |
| **Act 4 — The Hollow Moon** (*later*) | Day 40 | The moon goes dark again. Nina and the people she gathered return to the Arch. |

**The Seven Pieces:** Brennan's *Circlet*, Seraphina's *Confession*, Elyra's *Moonveil Key*, Kai's *Moonstone Chip*, Finn's *Honest Book*, Thorin's *Schematic*, Raika's *Hakon's Echo*. When all seven are gathered, Nina can rebuild the **Moonstone** and attempt the re-sealing. Who stands with her depends on who she's helped, who she told, and who she hurt.

## 8. Ending sketch (later)

- **The Mend:** re-seal the Hush properly using Ysolde's sacrifice (Ysolde wakes, but the lamps go out for good).
- **The Share:** divide the Hush among the people who love Nina (costly, warm, the *cozy* ending).
- **The Thaw:** free Ysolde and release the Hush into the sea (it will not be gentle).
- **The Lion's Price:** give the Order what it wants (the dark ending; the Realm gets a new winter).
